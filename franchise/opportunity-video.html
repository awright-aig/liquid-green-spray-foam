Own the Business. Own the Time. Own the Future.

If you've been thinking about a business that pays you back in more than a paycheck, the Liquid Green Spray Foam franchise is worth a serious look. This is an owner operator business — you run the company, you build the team, you set the standard, and the work you do every day directly affects the bottom line. No corporate ladder. No glass ceiling. No waiting for someone else to decide your ceiling. You make the calls.

One of the most underrated benefits of franchise ownership is controlling your time. The mobile rig and the protected territory mean your day is yours to shape — start early, finish early, take a long weekend, attend your kid's game, or run the business in the evenings and let the crew do the production. You're not punching a clock. You're building something that lets you choose how you spend your hours.

And the work itself is genuinely meaningful. Spray foam is the highest-R-value insulation per inch of any common product, and the demand is structural — stricter building codes, rising energy costs, and a homeowner base that increasingly wants high-performance envelopes. You are part of the green movement: every job you spray is a home that uses less energy, costs less to heat and cool, and is more comfortable to live in. Every project is an investment in energy efficient homes for families and businesses in your community. The product is good and the work is good — both of those are increasingly rare.

The investment opportunity is real. Total startup is in the $136,000 to $194,000 range — the mobile spray foam rig, equipment, initial inventory, 20-hour Liquid Green Spray Academy, 1–2 week HQ onboarding in Brewster NY, brand assets, CRM, and working capital for the first 90 days. Treat the range as a planning benchmark — actual startup cost varies by rig configuration, territory size, and operator circumstances. Results vary by operator and market. The full Item 7 lives in the Franchise Kit.

You don't need spray foam experience. Most of our operators come from sales, military, manufacturing, HVAC, project management, or other trades. The 20-hour Spray Academy covers the technical side, the HQ onboarding covers the operating system, and the protected territory is yours. Got foam?
