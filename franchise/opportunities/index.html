The Liquid Green Spray Foam Franchise Opportunity

Liquid Green is one of the fastest-growing spray foam insulation brands in the United States. We're expanding into new markets by selling franchises to operators who want to own a local business in the construction, energy efficiency, and home-services economy.

Spray foam insulation is a $13.6 billion U.S. market (per IBISWorld) growing at high single digits. Demand is driven by stricter building codes, rising energy costs, and homeowner awareness. The installer base is fragmented and aging — there is real room for new operators. Our market data goes deeper; the short version is: this is a ground-floor entry in a market that needs operators.

Frequently Asked Questions

Franchise Opportunities — Common Questions

How much does a Liquid Green franchise cost?

Initial investment ranges from $136,000 to $194,000, which includes the franchise fee, the mobile spray foam rig, equipment, initial inventory, training, and working capital. Liquid Green's fee structure is transparent — no hidden marketing co-op deductions, no required national ad spend tiers. See the Franchise Kit for the full Item 7 cost breakdown.

What territories are currently available?

Available territories in 2026 include: a multi-county territory in Western Connecticut (Fairfield, Litchfield, and New Haven counties), the Lehigh Valley (Allentown PA area), and statewide development rights in New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Texas, Florida, Oklahoma, and Connecticut. The Brewster NY branch is the fully operational flagship — a reference model for new operators.

Do I need construction or spray foam experience?

No. Most of our franchisees come from outside the trades — sales, military, manufacturing, HVAC, project management. The 20-hour Liquid Green Spray Academy covers the technical side, and the 1–2 week HQ onboarding in Brewster covers the operational side: rig setup, scheduling, customer acquisition, and the first jobs. The operating system is the product, not just the foam.

What kind of work will I do day to day?

Local work — every job is in your protected territory. Your week is a mix of estimating and bidding, scheduling installs, supervising the crew, and managing customer relationships. You run the business; the rig and the system do the production work. Most operators spend 60% of their time on sales and operations, 30% on production oversight, and 10% on admin.

How do I get more information?

Three steps: (1) request the free Franchise Kit, (2) review it and book a 30-minute call with our franchise team, (3) if it's a fit, we'll set up a market visit and reference conversations with existing operators. The full FDD is shared after the call.
