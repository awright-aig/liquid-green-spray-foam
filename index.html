The Process

From inquiry to open in 120–180 days.

Four steps. We don’t bury the path. Each step ends with a clear decision point so you know where you are.

1

Education & Fit

Submit the inquiry form. We talk — 30 minutes, no script. You learn about the brand, the investment, the day-to-day of a Liquid Green operator, and the financial requirements. We learn whether you’re a fit for the model. If yes, we send the Franchise Kit.

2

FDD & Validation

You receive the full Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD), including the Item 7 investment breakdown, Item 12 territory description, and the Franchise Agreement. We strongly encourage you to validate the model with existing operators and a franchise attorney. Take the time you need.

3

Discovery Day

Visit Brewster. Tour the working branch. Meet the team, see the equipment, walk the playbook. We cover the marketing stack (CRM, AIG partnership, lead routing) and lock in your territory boundaries in person. AIG joins for the marketing & tech consultation.

4

Approval & Launch

Execute the Franchise Agreement. Complete the 20-hour Spray Academy (two attendees at no extra charge). Spend 1–2 weeks onboarding at Brewster HQ. Set up your local presence, vehicle wrap, and CRM. Open your territory with the full playbook — 120–180 days from signed agreement.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Liquid Green franchise cost?

Total initial investment ranges from $136,000 to $194,000. This covers the mobile spray foam rig, proportioner and gun package, initial inventory of open-cell and closed-cell foam and coatings, branding and vehicle wrap, the 20-hour Liquid Green Spray Academy, 1–2 weeks of HQ onboarding in Brewster, CRM access, the operating playbook, and working capital for the first 90 days. Request the Franchise Kit for the full Item 7 breakdown.

Do I need prior spray foam experience?

No. The Liquid Green franchise is designed for first-time operators. Our 20-hour Spray Academy covers equipment, application, sales, operations, marketing, and compliance. Two attendees train at no additional charge.

How long from inquiry to open?

Typically 120–180 days from signed agreement to grand opening, depending on territory readiness, equipment delivery, and your local market setup. Most operators complete onboarding and are scheduling their first jobs within 90 days of training.

What does the protected territory look like?

Each franchisee is granted a protected territory defined by a 50-mile radius around an approved location. No other Liquid Green operator will be placed inside that boundary. Territory boundaries are confirmed during the Discovery Day process and locked in the Franchise Agreement.

What are the ongoing fees?

Per the FDD: a $300/month Technology/CRM Fee for the required CRM platform, a $300/month National Marketing Fund contribution for customer lead generation and brand awareness, and a minimum 1% of Gross Revenues for local advertising and promotion in your territory. Optional AIG local marketing services are available at $3,200/month under a separate agreement (not part of the Franchise Agreement). Royalty and Brand fee structure is disclosed in the FDD.

Is the Brewster NY branch for sale?

Yes. The Brewster NY branch is a working, revenue-generating operation and is available for transfer to a new operator. The sales structure differs from a standard franchise territory because the branch is already creating revenue — pricing is set on a case-by-case basis. Contact the owner directly to discuss.
