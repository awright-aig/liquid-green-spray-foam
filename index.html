A note from Harvey

Built by one family. Still run by one.

I started Liquid Green in 2010 out of our garage in Brewster. We’ve grown since then — but we’re still the same family. When you call, you get us. When we’re on the job, it’s our crew. That hasn’t changed.

We work in homes, barns, additions, and commercial buildings across the Hudson Valley — Putnam, Dutchess, Westchester, and the surrounding counties. We’re not the biggest spray foam company. We’re the one that picks up the phone, shows up on time, and leaves the site cleaner than we found it.

— Harvey Barnes, Founder & Owner

What we do

Spray foam for every kind of building.

Open cell and closed cell foam, installed by our own crew. We don’t sub out, we don’t cut corners, and we don’t leave until the job is right.

Homes & Additions

New construction, attics, rim joists, basements, and bonus rooms. We work with your builder or directly with you.

Commercial & Light Industrial

Pre-drywall commercial, warehouses, agricultural, and selective light-industrial work.

Barns & Outbuildings

Pole barns, equestrian, hobby shops, and outbuildings. A big part of what we do in the Hudson Valley.
