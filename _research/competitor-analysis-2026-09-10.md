# Competitor Franchise Site Analysis

**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Sites reviewed:** acehandymanfranchising.com, koalainsulationfranchise.com, usainsulationfranchise.com
**Method:** Search snippets + third-party audits + FDD data. Direct fetch of all three sites timed out (archon-server / file load failed). Data triangulated from franchisegator.com, franchisechatter.com, commonfranchise.com, and search results.

## Ace Handyman Services (acehandymanfranchising.com)

**Positioning:** 90-year Ace Hardware legacy. "Join a 90-year tradition of doing things right."

**Hero claim (above the fold):**
- SINGLE TERRITORY — $775K+ AUV, $49K Owner Discretionary Income (~6%)
- MULTI-UNIT OWNERS — $1.5M+ AUV, $223K Owner Discretionary Income (~15%)

**Above-the-fold CTA:** zip code input (not a form). Direct "SUBMIT" — 5 fields, low friction.

**Header:** vanity phone (866-FIX-MY-HOME), "GET AN ESTIMATE" button.

**Other elements:**
- "An Ace Hardware Company" trust signal
- Branded red van imagery (technician stepping out of van)
- Localized sub-pages (e.g., "Experienced Handyman Services in Dallas, TX")
- SMS lead capture (Name, Mobile Phone, Message) — text-based
- 5-star rating emphasis

**Financial (FDD 2026):**
- Franchise fee: $70K–$100K
- Total investment: $132K–$226K
- Territory size: 70K–100K households
- Royalty: 6%, Brand fee: 2%
- 305 franchised outlets (170 different owners) as of 2022

**Strengths to copy:** Zip-code-as-CTA, concrete income numbers above the fold, vanity phone, "An Ace Hardware Company" parent-brand trust.

**Weaknesses:** Homepage is heavy on claims, light on education (no video, no steps to ownership visible). Below-the-fold content is minimal. Site is more lead-gen than education.

## Koala Insulation (koalainsulationfranchise.com)

**Positioning:** "Start Your Own Insulation Business" — mobile, territory-based, low entry.

**5-step "How to own":**
1. Explore the Opportunity
2. Review the Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD)
3. Meet with Our Team (Discovery Day)
4. Secure Financing
5. Launch Your Business

**Territory map:** detailed city-level listing ("Available Markets: Alexander City, AL; Athens, AL; Auburn, AL..."). Some markets noted with multiple territories ("Fairfield, CA (2 territories)").

**Disclaimer (prominent on territory page):**
> "This website and the franchise sales information on this site do not constitute an offer to sell a franchise. The offer of a franchise can only be made through the delivery of a Franchise Disclosure Document. Certain states require that we register the franchise disclosure document in those states."

**Financial:**
- Total investment: $182,558–$234,272 (low end), sometimes listed $189K–$234K
- Net worth: $1M, Equity: $150K–$200K
- Liquid capital: not specified

**Strengths to copy:** Concrete 5-step process, city-level territory list, FDD disclaimer pattern, "Discovery Day" terminology that candidates can search for.

**Weaknesses:** No concrete income claim on homepage. No testimonials. No day-in-the-life content. Light on trust signals.

## USA Insulation (usainsulationfranchise.com)

**Positioning:** "Own a Home & Business Insulation Franchise." "Proprietary product, large territories, incredible earnings potential."

**4-step "Steps to Ownership":**
1. **Education and Fit** — talk with us, hear about a day in the life of an owner, learn about financial startup and potential earnings, discover ideal franchisee traits
2. **Franchise Disclosure Document and Validation** — review FDD, speak with franchise owners across the country, understand attributes of success, determine fit with potential peers
3. **Discovery Day** — attend Discovery Day, meet the support team, learn about the Threshold Brands Advantage (parent company)
4. **Mutual Decision to Move Forward & Launch** — execute franchise documents, start onboarding and launch process

**Territory map:** color-coded. Three statuses:
- Available
- Limited availability
- No availability

**Territory sizing (specific):**
- Large Market: up to 150K homes built before 1990
- Small Market: up to 100K Older Homes
- Two types of territories explicitly named

**Financial (FDD 2026):**
- Total investment: $265,500–$410,000 (some sources say $304K–$477K)
- Liquid capital: $100K
- Net worth: $300K
- Franchise fee: $45,000–$55,000
- 119 franchised units, 19 states active

**Trust signals:**
- VetFran 5-star member, 20% off franchise fee for veterans
- Newsroom (press releases: "USA Insulation Honors Minnesota Vietnam Veteran with Free Home Insulation," "Mike Kegley expands footprint with two additional units in Louisville")
- "Threshold Brands Advantage" (parent company)

**Strengths to copy:** 4-step process, color-coded territory map, concrete territory sizing, veteran discount, newsroom for press.

**Weaknesses:** Heavy on legalese, no zip lookup, no founder photo, no "day in the life" content embedded in the page (only mentioned as a step).

## Top 5 Recommendations (Ranked by Impact vs. Effort)

### 1. Add a concrete income/ROI claim to the franchise hero (HIGH IMPACT, LOW EFFORT)

Ace's hero is $223K Owner Discretionary Income. USA's is "incredible earnings potential." Koala's is muted. Liquid Green has nothing.

**What to add:** "Average operator $XXXK+ revenue in year one" or "Break-even in 6-9 months" or "Owner-operator income $XX-$XXK range" — only if defensible per FDD Item 19.

If the actual FDD doesn't disclose, use proxy metrics that ARE public:
- "20-hour training + 1-2 weeks HQ onboarding = operators start jobs in month 1" (already have this framing, promote harder)
- "Avg 12-20 jobs/month at 2-truck scale" (already in the Brewster opportunity page copy)
- "A working branch: 1-2 person crew, no office staff required to start" (mobile model)

**Where:** Hero, below the H1 + lead.

### 2. City-level territory listing with availability status (HIGH IMPACT, MEDIUM EFFORT)

Koala and USA both list specific cities by state with "Available" / "Limited" / "No availability" status. Liquid Green has a US map only.

**What to add:** A territory detail page (or section on the franchise page) listing:
- For each state: which cities/regions are Available, Limited, or Reserved
- Click a state on the map → expands to show the cities
- "Open" / "Coming soon" / "Reserved" badges per city

**Where:** Expand the existing US map (line 408) — currently only shows state hover/click. Add a panel below that fills in with the city detail when a state is clicked.

### 3. "Day in the Life of a Liquid Green Owner" content (HIGH IMPACT, MEDIUM EFFORT)

USA mentions "day in the life" as a step but doesn't deliver. None of the three do this well. Liquid Green has the Brewster HQ story to anchor it.

**What to add:** A blog post + franchise page section showing:
- A typical week: estimate on Monday, install Tue-Thu, follow-up Friday, weekend admin (already on the Brewster page — promote it)
- A day-in-the-life video (could be 2-3 min) of the Brewster operator doing a real job
- Operator testimonial: a 60-second clip from a real owner talking about the job

**Where:** New section on /franchise/ between "How It Works" and the form. Plus a "Meet the Operators" page at /operators/.

### 4. Add explicit Liquid Capital + Net Worth requirements (MEDIUM IMPACT, LOW EFFORT)

USA and Koala both state these. Liquid Green only states total investment.

**What to add:** Below the $136K-$194K line:
- "Liquid capital: $XK"
- "Net worth: $YK"
- "VetFran-friendly" (if Harvey does this)

**Where:** Hero/investment block on the franchise page, and the FAQ Q about cost.

### 5. FDD compliance disclaimer (HIGH IMPACT, LOW EFFORT — but legally required)

Koala has it. Liquid Green likely should too, especially because Brewster is for sale (regulatory nuance).

**What to add (footer or sidebar of the franchise page):**
> "This website and the franchise sales information on this site do not constitute an offer to sell a franchise. The offer of a franchise can only be made through the delivery of a Franchise Disclosure Document. Certain states require that we register the franchise disclosure document in those states before we can offer franchises in those states."

**Where:** Footer of /franchise/, /opportunities/, /kit, and /faqs.
